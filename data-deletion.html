Data Deletion Instructions

SwnW Social Publisher

This page explains how an authorised owner of a connected Meta business account can request deletion of data held by SwnW Social Publisher.

Request deletion

Send an email to mohamed.abdelaal@swnwclinics.com with the subject “Meta Data Deletion Request”. Include:

  1. The Facebook Page name or Instagram Professional account username
  2. The email address of the Meta account owner or administrator
  3. A request to remove the SwnW Social Publisher connection

What we delete

After we verify the request, we remove application-held connection identifiers, publishing credentials, and associated publishing records that are no longer needed for security, legal, or accounting obligations.

Timing

We acknowledge the request and provide a confirmation reference. We complete deletion within 30 days after verification, unless a longer retention period is required by law or needed to protect against fraud or security incidents.

Disconnecting through Meta

You can also remove the application directly from your Meta Business settings. Removing access stops future publishing. You can still email us if you also want application-held records deleted.
