Privacy Policy for SwnW Social Publisher

Last updated: 21 September 2026

SwnW Social Publisher is an internal content-management tool used by authorised SwnW staff to schedule and publish approved content to connected Facebook Pages and Instagram Professional accounts.

Information we process

When an authorised business user connects a Meta asset, the application processes only the information needed to publish and track content:

  • Facebook Page and Instagram Professional account identifiers
  • Page publishing permission and server-side access credentials
  • Post media, captions, selected publishing destinations, schedules, publishing results, and post links
  • Internal staff account details, roles, and activity logs needed to control access and audit publishing

How we use this information

We use this information only to connect approved business accounts, publish or schedule approved content, show publishing status, retain an internal audit trail, and investigate publishing errors.

We do not use Meta data for advertising to individuals. The publishing feature does not access Facebook or Instagram users’ private messages, profile data, contacts, or patient medical records.

Storage and security

Publishing credentials are kept in server-side configuration and are not exposed to dashboard browsers. Access to publishing actions is limited to authorised staff. We apply role-based access controls and keep activity records to protect the connected accounts and investigate unauthorised use.

Sharing

We share the minimum information required to publish content with Meta through the Meta Graph API. We do not sell Meta data or share it with unrelated third parties.

Retention

We keep connection and publishing records while the business account remains connected and for as long as reasonably necessary for operational, security, and legal record-keeping purposes. An authorised account owner can ask us to remove the connection and associated application data.

Your choices and data deletion

An authorised account owner may remove this application from Meta Business settings or request deletion of application-held Meta connection data. See our Data Deletion Instructions.
